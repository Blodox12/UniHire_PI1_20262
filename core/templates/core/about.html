About UniHire

UniHire helps university students obtain their first professional experience by matching them with internships and entry-level opportunities based on academic knowledge, technical skills, certifications, and interests — instead of prior work experience.

Companies can post openings, review applicants, and manage the hiring process, while students can search, filter, and apply to roles that fit their profile in just a few clicks.
